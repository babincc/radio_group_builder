## 0.1.0 - October 4, 2026

- Initial release
- Build labeled choices on Flutter's native RadioGroup (Flutter 3.35+).
- Add typed RadioGroupBuilder and ChangeNotifier-based RadioGroupController.
- Support vertical and horizontal layouts, widget/custom labels, decoration,
  toggleable selection, and disabled groups or individual choices.
- Add value/index selection, silent updates, and controller lifecycle handling.
- Include a modular V2-style example and migration guide from radio_group_v2.
